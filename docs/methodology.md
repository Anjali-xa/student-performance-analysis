# Student Performance Analysis Methodology

## Measures
- Mean and median score by subject and assessment.
- Pass rate using the institution's documented pass threshold.
- Attendance rate using attended sessions / eligible sessions.
- Score distribution by cohort and assessment period.
- Change in score across comparable assessments, where student matching is valid.

## Validation
- Confirm score ranges and assessment maximums.
- Check student IDs for duplicates and nulls.
- Standardise subject names and assessment dates.
- Distinguish absent, excused, and zero-score records.
- Verify attendance denominators and enrolment dates.
- Check that cohort comparisons use equivalent assessments.

## Responsible use
- Use anonymised student identifiers.
- Do not publish identifiable student-level records.
- Avoid ranking individuals without context.
- Treat subgroup differences as descriptive, not proof of cause.
- Document small-group suppression rules where applicable.

## Questions
1. How do score distributions vary by subject?
2. Is attendance associated with assessment outcomes?
3. How do cohorts compare across equivalent assessments?
4. Which areas may warrant further educational investigation?
